# Product Overview

## What SSOT Platform Is

SSOT is an enterprise content intelligence and provenance platform that unifies distributed digital content into a single canonical index using deterministic identity (BLAKE3 + UUID7).

## Core Value

- deduplicates content across sources
- preserves immutable lineage and movement history
- supports semantic grouping beyond exact byte matches
- enables forensic-grade capture and verification workflows
- transforms conversational data into ranked ideas, tasks, and insights

## Target Users

- enterprise IT and digital workplace teams
- security and investigation units
- compliance and records governance teams
- platform engineering and data architecture teams

## Product Modules

1. Ingestion and provider sync
2. Canonical identity and versioning
3. Duplicate and move intelligence
4. Semantic similarity (future intelligence layer)
5. DesktopCam forensic recorder (for regulated evidence workflows)
6. Global chat ingestion and idea intelligence layer

## Business Positioning

- licensable platform
- modular integration model
- enterprise deployment friendly
- audit-ready architecture suitable for due diligence
